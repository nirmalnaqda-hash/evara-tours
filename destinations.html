Must-Visit Places & Activities

Discover the top attractions and exciting adventures Sri Lanka has to offer.

Popular Tourist Spots

Handpicked heritage, highland, and coastal wonderlands.

Sigiriya & Dambulla

Step back in time at the 8th wonder of the world. Sigiriya features ancient frescoes, lion paw gateway, and magnificent summit views. Nearby Dambulla Cave Temple showcases over 150 gold Buddha statues.

Best time: All Year

Ella & Hill Country

Enjoy cool mountain breezes, iconic train rides through emerald tea plantations, Little Adam's Peak hikes, and the famous Nine Arch Bridge architectural marvel.

Best time: Jan - August

Yala & Udawalawe Wildlife

Experience thrilling 4x4 open jeep safaris! Home to the highest density of leopards in the world, herds of wild Asian elephants, crocodiles, and exotic birds.

Best time: Feb - July

Mirissa & South Coast Beaches

Golden palm-fringed beaches, vibrant nightlife, world-class surfing waves, Coconut Tree Hill photoshoots, and blue whale watching expeditions in the deep ocean.

Best time: Nov - April

Fun Activities You Can Do

Create unforgettable memories with action-packed adventures.

Whale Watching & Surfing

Witness giant Blue Whales in Mirissa and ride Atlantic-grade waves in Arugam Bay or Weligama.

Scenic Kandy-Ella Train

Ride one of the most beautiful train journeys on earth, crossing mist-covered tea gardens and viaducts.

Hiking & Trekking

Conquer Adam's Peak, Horton Plains World's End, or Little Adam's Peak for panoramic views.

Tea Tasting & Factory Tour

Walk through historical Ceylon tea factories and sample authentic world-renowned Pure Ceylon Tea.

Authentic Spice & Cooking Class

Learn Sri Lankan rice and curry cooking methods using organic local spices and coconut milk.

Ayurvedic Wellness & Spa

Rejuvenate your body and soul with herbal oils, ancient Ayurveda massages, and yoga retreats.
